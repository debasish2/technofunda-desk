How to read this table

One row per session. 4R is stocks up 4% or more over stocks down more than 4%, 20R and 50R are stocks above over stocks below their 20 and 50-day average (all times 100), and each Change is that ratio's change from the day before. New 52-week High / Low are the shares of stocks making a new 52-week high or low. Green and red cells follow the usual thresholds (4R above 200 or below 50; 20R above 75 or below 50; 50R above 85 or below 60; changes above +20 or below −20). A day is green when green cells outnumber red by 3 or more, red when red outnumber green by 3 or more; a day with 3 or more red cells that is not red is a warning day.
